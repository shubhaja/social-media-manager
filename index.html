Shubha's Social Media Manager

A small personal tool that Shubha Jagannatha (@shubhastudios) uses to post their own videos to their own accounts on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and X.

How it works

Shubha edits a video and its caption on their own computer, picks which accounts it should go to, and schedules it. At the chosen time, the tool uploads the video and caption to those accounts through each platform's official API.

It only ever posts to accounts Shubha owns and has signed in to. It isn't offered to other people, it doesn't read anyone else's content, and it doesn't collect data about viewers.

Privacy Policy

Shubha's Social Media Manager · last updated November 11, 2026

Shubha's Social Media Manager is a personal tool used only by its owner, Shubha Jagannatha, to publish their own videos to their own social media accounts.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok or X, the tool receives an access token for that account. It uses the token only to upload videos and captions the owner chose, and to read basic account details (account name and ID) and the status of those posts.

What it stores

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and in the owner's private cloud storage. Videos waiting to be posted are kept in private cloud storage and deleted automatically after they're posted or within 21 days.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. It doesn't access other users' accounts or content, and it doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected apps settings. Questions can be sent through the contact details on the owner's Instagram profile, @shubhastudios.
